I'm Chris Smith. I live in the San Francisco Bay Area and build software, mostly around AI agents, infrastructure, distributed systems and data. I have an endless appetite for making things in both the digital and physical worlds.

With a background in engineering and psychology, and as a Zen practitioner, I'm drawn to the intersection of AI, cognitive psychology and contemplative practice. As we build minds in our own image, can we come to understand ourselves better in the process? What does it really mean for a model or a person to know something? What is the essential “self” behind our thoughts and feelings, and how should that shape where we take AI next? And if we created something with awareness, how would we even know? I think answering these questions is essential to developing technology in ways that enhance the human experience.

Since 2023 I've been on a career break, working independently on my own projects.
