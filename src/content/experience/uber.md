As one of Uber's first data engineers, I helped build its first in-house realtime data system and scaled it through the hypergrowth years as it expanded across the world. I moved into management, then shifted to developer experience, and co-founded and led Mindfulness@, an employee group that offered meditation spaces and sessions at offices around the world.
